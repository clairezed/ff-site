About

About Northstar Digital

We are nine people in Berlin and Lisbon who design and build digital products for mid-sized European companies. Independent since 2014, deliberately small, and still run by the two people who started it.

Why the studio exists

Most of our clients have been through one failed rebuild before they call us.

Northstar Digital started in 2014 because two senior people were tired of being sold into projects they then never touched. The pattern was always the same: an experienced team wins the work, and a much less experienced team delivers it.

So the studio is built the other way round. The people in your first call are the people who do the work, and we cap the number of concurrent projects rather than the number of hours we can bill. It means we turn down more work than we take, and it is the reason clients come back.

  • Mission Make digital products that a mid-sized company can own, extend and afford to run for a decade.
  • Scope over speed We would rather spend two weeks proving the problem than six months building the wrong answer quickly.
  • No growth for its own sake Nine people is a deliberate ceiling. Beyond that we would need managers between you and the work.
Values

Four things we do not negotiate

  • Small team, senior only

    Every person on your project has shipped production work for a decade or more. Nothing is handed to a junior once the contract is signed.

  • Evidence over opinion

    We instrument before we redesign. Decisions reference analytics, support tickets and user sessions rather than taste.

  • Plain language

    No jargon in status updates, no surprise invoices, and a written summary after every workshop so decisions are traceable.

  • Built to be handed over

    You get documented code, a design system your team can extend and a maintenance plan. Lock-in is not a business model we like.

History

How the studio got here

  1. 2014

    Founded as a two-person studio

    Marta and Ruben leave a large agency to build software for mid-sized German industrial firms, working out of a shared studio in Kreuzberg.

  2. 2017

    First platform engagement

    Atlas Logistics asks for a dispatcher tool rather than a website. The team grows to five and adds dedicated backend engineering.

  3. 2020

    Design systems practice

    Remote work pushes clients toward shared component libraries. We formalise the design system practice that now underpins most engagements.

  4. 2023

    Performance and accessibility as contract terms

    We start writing Core Web Vitals and WCAG conformance into statements of work, with budgets enforced in continuous integration.

  5. 2026

    Nine specialists, two studios

    Berlin and Lisbon, one delivery team. Still no account managers between you and the people doing the work.
