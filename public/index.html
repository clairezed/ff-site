Process

How a project actually runs

Four phases, each ending with something you can review. Nothing is invoiced against a milestone you cannot see.

  1. Discovery

    Two weeks of interviews, analytics review and a technical audit. You leave with a written problem statement and a prioritised scope.

    1–2 weeks

  2. Design

    Wireframes, then a component-level design system. We prototype the three journeys that carry the business case before anything is built.

    2–4 weeks

  3. Build

    Two-week increments on a staging URL you can use from day one. Accessibility and performance budgets are checked in CI, not at the end.

    6–14 weeks

  4. Launch and iterate

    Migration, monitoring and a four-week hardening window. Then a quarterly review of what the data says to change next.

    Ongoing

Questions

Before you get in touch

How quickly can you start?
Discovery work usually starts within two to three weeks. Full delivery teams are planned a quarter ahead, so a summer start is typically agreed in spring.
Do you work with in-house teams?
Often. Roughly half of our engagements are mixed teams where we provide design and frontend while your engineers own the backend. We work in your repository, your review process and your tracker.
Which industries do you know well?
Energy, healthcare, logistics and industrial manufacturing, plus a long tail of professional services. If your domain is regulated or your users are not consumers, we have probably worked next to it.
Are you a website studio or a software studio?
Both, and the distinction matters less every year. Roughly half our revenue is marketing sites and half is internal tooling and customer platforms.
